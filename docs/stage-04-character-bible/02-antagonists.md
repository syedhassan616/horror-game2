# 02 — The Antagonists

**None of them is evil.** Each is written so that a reasonable player agrees with them at
least once. If a draft makes any of them wrong-and-knows-it, the draft is rejected.

---

## VELLUM — the clerk

> *"I am going to need you to hold still, and also to stop being like that."*

**History.** Field clerk, Registry, Assay division. **Not a construct** — a person, with a
lanyard, a salary, a flat, and a mother in Verrick. Nine years in post. Best audit-closure
rate in the division. Fought four times.

**Goals.** *Today:* close the audit. *Overall:* be good at this, because he is, and because
being good at it is the only defence he has against what it is.

**Fears.** That there is no box for it. Literally: his crisis is procedural before it is
moral. He does not think *"this is wrong"*; he thinks *"I cannot file this,"* and it takes
him four fights to notice those are the same sentence.

**Personality.** Formal, dry, unexpectedly funny, deeply conscientious. Apologises for
procedural delays and means it. **He is the character the community will make fan art of,
and he is funny for two hours before he is anything else.**

**Dialogue style.** Cites item numbers. Speaks in numbered clauses when nervous — and gets
*more* numbered as he becomes less certain, so his composure and his citation density are
inversely correlated and audibly so.

**Arc across four fights.**

| Fight | State | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (Act I mini) | Flawless. Funny. Bureaucratically menacing. | Mirror tether at 0.7s, perfect |
| 2 (Act II, VRK-15) | Rattled. **Apologises to a creature mid-audit, then corrects himself** (F09). Present for the Slack Tether. | First hesitation frames |
| 3 (Act III) | Openly bad at his job. Patterns develop visible gaps. | Mirror at 1.1s; sometimes doesn't fire |
| 4 (Finale-adjacent) | **He has grown a Ward — for Aven.** | Itemises every mercy Aven showed him, aloud, as a numbered list, because it is the only register he has |

> **VELLUM.** "Item four. At Verrick, second audit, you did not pursue when I withdrew,
> and I have been unable to file that." *(beat)* "There isn't a box."

**Route outcomes.** Defects (CARRY) · Final Audit, does his job, and it is awful (LEDGER) ·
promoted (DRIFT) · replaced by Quill if the player pushed Quill in (Quest 22).

**Battle mechanic.** The only enemy with a tether of his own. It mirrors Aven's movement
one companion-behaviour behind. **Fighting Vellum is fighting your own last hour of play.**

**Theme.** *A Clerk, Ascending* — the same 8 bars, transposed up a semitone per encounter,
arrangement losing an instrument each time it rises.

## SISTER ANNIKE — the kind room

> *"Nobody here was taken. Everybody here asked."*

**Design brief:** *write the antagonist the player cannot beat in an argument.*

**History.** Nineteen years at Hushfell, the last eleven running the rite. Has performed
**three hundred** severances personally, because she will not ask anyone else to carry it.
Filed a bulk petition in autumn 902 (Petition II) to cut a nine-week queue in which people
were dying.

**Goals.** *Today:* Ossa's third day, correctly. *Overall:* that nobody waits in a queue
for relief they have already asked for.

**Fears.** That one of the three hundred would have changed their mind on day four.

**Personality.** Unhurried. Warm without being soft. Has stopped finding any of it
difficult, which is the most frightening thing about her and which she knows.

**Dialogue style.** **She never argues. She answers.** Every objection Aven raises, she has
heard, gives its due, and remains standing there having done it three hundred times.
**Tell: she never speeds up.** Her babble rate does not change in any scene in the game,
including her own boss fight.

**Arc.** Certainty → the Rendering (LEDGER), where she learns what her efficiency became →
*"I would do it again for the three hundred. I have no idea what to do about the rest,
and I have been standing here for two days."*

**Battle mechanic.** **Fights defensively only.** Never attacks. Parries, withdraws, talks.
Killing her is trivial and **permanently closes the True Ending**, unwarned. The Register
gains a 301st entry in her hand: *"I have been asked a question I can't file."*

**Favourite things.** The kettle rota. Being contradicted competently. Bread with too much
salt in it, which she makes badly on purpose because her mother did.

## THE CANTOR — the forty-first part

> *"A thing that is being used cannot be filed. We are in use. Continuously. That is the
> entire theology."*

**History.** Leads the Drowned Choir; the eleventh Cantor since Year 288. Has never
surfaced. Real name exists in the fiction and is spoken **once**, on an inscription in the
Keeping, and never aloud.

**Goals.** Hold the harmony. Keep the place set for whoever is coming.

**Fears.** That nobody is coming, and that the correct response to that is still to sing.

**Personality.** Certain, generous, immovable. Speaks in the plural — *we*, always, even
about personal matters — which is either communal or a symptom depending on the scene.

**Arc.** Holding → the Ward the choir cannot protect Sennet from → letting it end, or not.

**Battle mechanic.** **No health bar. Forty voices.** Each SEVER silences one, permanently,
on the save file, removing it from the region's diegetic mix for the remaining hours of the
game. UNKNOT requires convincing voices to stop *willingly* — which requires knowing their
individual stories, gathered beforehand in the overworld.

**Nothing warns the player that damage is permanent.**

## THE ASSAYER / MERIT VANE

> *"Grief is the interest on love. I have been forgiving the debt."*

**Two characters in one shell.** They must be written separately and never blended.

### The machine
- **Vocabulary:** intake only. Never says *kill, take, remove, hurt, help, save, mercy,*
  or *sorry.* Its cruellest lines are its most procedural.
- **Has never lied**, in nine hundred years or in six hours of play.
- **Cannot want anything.** Any line implying desire is a bug.
- **Babble: zero pitch variance.** Metronomic. The only character in the game whose speech
  has no expressive dimension at all.
- **Its rage phase is efficiency**, not anger — ninety seconds of total silence and maximum
  density. It does not get louder. It gets *better*.
- **It is funny once**, in phase 1, and the joke is a form joke, and it does not know it
  has made one.

### The person in the shell
Very old, technically alive, has not been looked at in nine centuries. Courteous. Answers
every question, including the hard ones, because there is nothing in them that would want
to avoid it.

> **AVEN.** "Do you regret it?"
> **MERIT.** *(the machine consults itself)* "I have the memory of intending not to."
> *(beat)* "That is the closest structure I have to an answer, and I am aware it is not one."

**Merit's history, goals, fears, and culpability:** Story Bible §02.3 and §10.2.

**Theme.** *The Ledger Line* — L2 ostinato as foundation; melodic material is **L1 played
backwards**. 7/8 under a 4/4 choir; they align exactly once, in phase 5.

## THE FIRST WARD

> *"He said he'd be an hour."*

**History.** Entry 1. The first Ward ever formed, in Nell's chest, Year 5, for Corrin Vane.
Never released by reunion, never ended by his death, never severed. Merit could not put it
in a drawer. It has been in a doorway for eight hundred and ninety-eight years.

**Goals.** Be able to say she waited.

**Fears.** None that translate. It has outlasted fear.

**Personality.** It is not malicious and barely hostile. **You are between it and the
door.** Everything it does follows from that single fact.

**Dialogue style.** Almost none. Barely speech — 1.2 syllables/sec, one sustained cello.
Its "lines" are pattern names the player reads on screen: *One More Hour. It's the Weather.
He Wouldn't. Please Check.*

**Battle mechanic.** Seven phases, no checkpoints, **24 patterns** in eight families of
three (GDD §08.6). Arena: one door-width wide, infinitely tall, never changes.

**The finisher.** SEVER works, and has always worked, and nine hundred years of nothing has
left it very tired. That ending is forty seconds long and is the worst thing in the game.

**UNKNOT** is *"It is holding a door,"* and the resolution is **to stop playing** — sixty
seconds of zero input, after which the door opens from the other side and Entry 2 comes
through: the part of Corrin that was walking home to her, removed before it arrived, and
filed.

**Theme.** *Still Waiting* — 44 BPM, solo cello, door-hinge percussion, 41-voice choir
entering only in phase 6 singing the 41st part. 9:40, one crescendo, no drop.
**L1's fifth note — withheld for the entire game — finally arrives at 8:52.**
