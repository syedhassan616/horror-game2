# 03 — World Overview

> Full region maps, room-by-room layouts, NPC placement schedules and prop lists are
> **Stage 3 (World Bible)**. This chapter fixes the geography, the atmosphere contract,
> and the content quota for each region.

---

## 3.1 Vesselmere at a Glance

Vesselmere is a **vertical** country. The Assayer sits at the top (the Assay), the
Keeping at the bottom, and the living world is stacked between them like sediment.
The world map is drawn as a **cross-section**, not a plan view — you always see how
far down the vault is, and how far up the machine is, and you are always in the middle.

**The four constants of every region:**

1. **A bell.** Every settlement has one. Its sound is that region's musical signature
   and its silence is that region's grief.
2. **A door with no keyhole.** One per region. Nine total. They are the Keeping.
3. **A Registry post.** Bureaucracy is the connective tissue. Forms travel between
   regions; so do your reputations.
4. **A person who is severed and doesn't mind.** The game's ambient argument, restated
   fourteen times by fourteen different people with fourteen different tones.

## 3.4 Region Interlock

The regions are not a corridor. Act II is **open in order** — the four regions can be
tackled in any sequence, and the game tracks which you did first:

- **First region visited in Act II** gets the "fresh" version of Vellum's audit
  (he is competent, formal, funny). Later regions get progressively rattled versions.
- **Last region visited in Act II** is where the Slack Tether set-piece fires,
  regardless of which it is — so the companion you lose depends on your route.
- Region difficulty **does not** scale. Doing the Drowned Choir first is genuinely
  harder, and the game rewards it with a unique Keepsake and one of Rue's best scenes.

## 3.5 Fast Travel & the World Map

- Fast travel unlocks per-region on **first bell rung**, not on region completion.
- The March is the hub and can be *called* to any region with a bell — but the March
  takes in-game time to arrive, and Act III has timed events. The convenience is a
  real trade.
- **The world map is a cross-section.** Regions sit at true depth. The Keeping is
  visible on the map from hour one as an unlabelled void beneath everything, and
  players *will* ask about it. It is never mentioned in dialogue until Act II.
