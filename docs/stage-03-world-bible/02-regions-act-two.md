# 02 — Regions II: The Salt Ledger → Lamplight March

---

## SLT · THE SALT LEDGER — 18 rooms

**Concept** A dried inland sea of powdered record-paper. Digging is reading.
**Palette** Bleached white / rust-orange shadow / violet horizon. **Forbidden colour** Blue
(water, once, and it is a mirage). **Music** *Everything Anyone Wrote Down*, 88 BPM.
**Weather** Salt-storms that **scour and reveal** — the layout genuinely changes.

| Room | Name | Class | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SLT-01 | The Shore That Was | Corridor | Heat shimmer introduced |
| SLT-02 | Coom's Camp | Chamber | Salt Sight tutorial. Quest 14. |
| SLT-03 | The Shallows | Set piece | Strata 1–4. Depth = date. |
| SLT-04 | The Salt Road | Corridor | First storm. Layout changes on exit. |
| SLT-05 | The Queue | Set piece | Mini-boss: the Dune Registrar's Queue |
| SLT-06 | Buried Street | Chamber | Archive-city upper level |
| SLT-07 | The Stamp Office | Chamber | The Dune Registrar. Quest 15. **Mirrors the First Ward.** |
| SLT-08 | The Deep Cut | Set piece | Strata 5–12. P-SLT-1: reconstruct filing order. |
| SLT-09 | Kell & Kell's Dig | Chamber | Quest 17. The most hopeful scene in the game. |
| SLT-10 | Filed Under Nothing | Set piece | Quest 16 ★. **Superboss chain step 5.** |
| SLT-11 | The Sorted Site | Chamber | Absence #8. Clean, stacked, no digger. |
| SLT-12 | The Tenth Stratum | **Arena** | Boss B4. Arena scrolls down through centuries. |
| SLT-13 | The Salt Bell | Chamber | Save/travel |
| SLT-14–18 | Dunes & cuts | Corridor ×5 | Strata 13–18; keyholeless door **#5** (SLT-16); 6 Keepsakes; Saltborne enemies |

**Storm system.** Three authored layouts for SLT-04/14/15/17/18. A storm fires on region
re-entry and on two scripted beats. **Two Keepsakes and one stratum are only reachable in
layout C**, which most players will see by accident on a third visit.

**Strata as lore delivery.** 18 collectibles, each a document fragment at a known depth.
Eight are the precedents of Story Bible §02.2. Reading them in *depth order* (which
requires digging down, not across) tells the nine-century story correctly; reading them in
*discovery order* tells it scrambled, which is how most players get it, which is the point.

**Keyholeless door response (SLT):** *"Free-standing in the salt. The drift has built up
against it on one side only, which would require wind that does not occur here."*

## VRK · VERRICK LOOMWORKS — 20 rooms

**Concept** A vertical factory city manufacturing synthetic Wards that fail at 41 days.
**Palette** Copper / bruised purple / sodium orange / steam-white.
**Forbidden colour** Sky blue (Dov's shopping-list paper). **Music** *Floor Nine*, 132 BPM.
**Weather** Indoor condensation rain; scheduled steam vents the player can learn.

| Room | Name | Class | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| VRK-02 | Floor Twelve — Intake | Chamber | 3 NPCs. Route-reactive shop. |
| VRK-03 | The Shaft | Set piece | Loomstep traversal tutorial |
| VRK-04 | Floor Nine | Chamber | **Barro.** S-221. The eyelet monologue. |
| VRK-05 | The Weaving Hall | Set piece | P-VRK-1: read the weave, predict the shift |
| VRK-06 | Night Shift Floor | Chamber | Absence #11 — a loom running correctly, unattended |
| VRK-07 | Quality Control | **Arena** | Mini-boss. Rejects you as defective. |
| VRK-08 | Idrisse's Office | Chamber | Quest 20 |
| VRK-09 | The Corridor | Chamber | Absence #9 — the complaints box. Quest 21. |
| VRK-10 | Materials | Set piece | Quest 18 ★ — the 41-day answer. **F07 payoff.** |
| VRK-11 | The Back Channel | Set piece | Where Keeping stock arrives. Nobody staffs it. |
| VRK-12 | Quill's Desk | Chamber | Quest 22 |
| VRK-13 | Barro's Bench | Chamber | The weave. S-226 (Choice B). |
| VRK-14 | Dov's House | Chamber | S-225. Absence #10. |
| VRK-15 | The Gantry | **Arena** | Boss B5 (Vellum) + **the Slack Tether** |
| VRK-16 | The Verrick Bell | Chamber | Save/travel. Cast in the works; it is slightly flat. |
| VRK-17–20 | Floors & stairs | Corridor ×4 | Keyholeless door **#6** (VRK-18); 6 Keepsakes; 12 Loomkin types |

**The steam schedule** is a 22-second loop, learnable, and used by three puzzles and one
optional Keepsake route. It is never explained. A wall chart in VRK-02 shows it, in the
factory's own notation, and the notation is decodable.

**VRK-11, the back channel**, is the room that proves Quest 18. It has no NPC, no
interaction beyond one manifest, and no music change. Players who find it before doing
Quest 18 have solved the mystery early, and the quest's dialogue *acknowledges this*
with a separate branch.

**Keyholeless door response (VRK):** *"Between the materials store and the wall. There is
a bench in front of it and the bench has been there long enough to leave marks."*

## CHR · THE DROWNED CHOIR — 14 rooms

**Concept** A flooded amphitheatre. 41 singers sank rather than be severed.
**Palette** Submarine teal / gold leaf / silt. **Forbidden colour** Any warm hue (the
surface, from below, at the end). **Music** *The Forty-First Part* — **diegetic**, 41 NPC
voices. **Weather** Caustics, particulate, current that pushes the player.

| Room | Name | Class | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CHR-01 | The Waterline | Corridor | Held Breath tutorial |
| CHR-02 | The Descent | Corridor | Music becomes audible before the choir is visible |
| CHR-03 | The Outer Ring | Chamber | 6 singers. Each has a name and a story. |
| CHR-04 | Sennet's Station | Chamber | Quest 23 |
| CHR-05 | The Undertow | **Arena** | Mini-boss |
| CHR-06 | The Chorister's Seat | Chamber | Quest 24. The tuneless singer. |
| CHR-07 | The Amphitheatre | Set piece | **Absence #12** — 41 seats, 40 occupied |
| CHR-08 | The Cantor's Deep | **Arena** | Boss B6 |
| CHR-09 | The Score | Set piece | Quest 25 ★. The 41st part, notated, resting. |
| CHR-10 | The Gold Room | Chamber | Sunken treasury; 3 Keepsakes |
| CHR-11 | The Choir Bell | Chamber | Save/travel. Rings underwater; sounds wrong. |
| CHR-12–14 | Flooded halls | Corridor ×3 | Keyholeless door **#7** (CHR-13); Sunken enemies |

**Diegetic music is the region's whole build.** Each of the 41 singers is an
`AudioStreamPlayer2D` positioned at their seat. The "soundtrack" is a spatial mix of NPCs.
Silencing one **destroys that node's stream reference on the save file** — there is no
re-enable path, by construction (GDD §11.6).

**Held Breath as a speech cost.** Underwater dialogue consumes air. Long conversations
require surfacing mid-scene, and the game lets conversations be *interrupted by drowning*,
which sounds like a failure state and is authored as a character beat: the NPC waits.

**Keyholeless door response (CHR):** *"Below the amphitheatre, and dry. The water stops
about a hand's width from it and does not explain itself."*

## MAR · LAMPLIGHT MARCH — 16 rooms (12 cars + 4)

**Concept** A caravan-city that has not stopped in sixty years. **The hub.**
**Palette** Warm lamplight against whatever biome scrolls past. **Forbidden colour** Black
(the twelfth car's interior). **Music** *Never Once Stopped*, 108 BPM.
**Weather** Whatever the parallax says — the March passes through every region's exterior.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**The March changes permanently by route** (GDD §03). Cars are added, sealed, or burned:

| Route | Car changes |
| --- | --- |
| CARRY | Car 13 added (returns processing, Vellum's desk) |
| LEDGER | Cars 9 and 10 sealed. Crew reduced to 4. Quest board halved. |
| DRIFT | Car 12 opened; the Undersleep accessible from it |

**Calling the March.** Fast travel *to* the March is instant; calling the March *to* a
region costs in-game time, and Act III has timed events. The convenience is a real trade
(GDD §03.5).

**Keyholeless door response (MAR):** *"On the underside of the third car, upside down
relative to the floor. It has been moving at speed for sixty years and there is no dust
on it."*
