# 02 — Barks, Interjections & Systemic Lines

The lines the player hears most often. These carry more of the game's character than any
cutscene, because they are heard hundreds of times.

---

## 7.2.2 Companion Interjections — ~340

Spoken during *other people's* scenes. The primary vehicle for a living party without
cutscenes.

| Trigger | Count | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NPC states something the companion knows is wrong | 48 | Tilly, to a bell-founder: *"That's not a sallie. That's a rope."* |
| Topic touches the companion's history | 62 | Barro goes quiet when severance is discussed casually. **The interjection is a `[BEAT]` with a portrait change and no words.** |
| Player picks a tone the companion dislikes | 55 | Rue, after BLUNT: *"…Right. Well. That's one way."* |
| Player picks a tone the companion likes | 55 | Sennet, after CAREFUL: nothing. He stays in frame one beat longer. |
| Scene mentions someone dead | 40 | Moth quotes them. |
| Region-specific | 80 | Tilly in Verrick: *"It's very loud. How does anyone hear the — oh. There isn't one."* |

**`Vouch`** (skill node) promotes one interjection per companion per encounter into a
selectable SPEAK option.

## 7.2.3 Enemy Lines — 84 × 3 minimum

Every enemy speaks. Three lines minimum, spoken during the Weave phase, positioned in
space with the enemy.

**The rule:** an enemy's lines must make its diagnosis *inferable* without stating it.

| Enemy | Lines | Correct diagnosis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Filing Error | *"Where does this go."* / *"This is not where this goes."* / *"Please."* | a category it doesn't fit |
| Cracked Peal | *"I was wrong once."* / *"At a bad time."* / *"I have been very careful since."* | a mistake it made once |
| Cooling-Off | *"Not yet."* / *"There's a day left."* / *"You can come back on Thursday."* | three days |
| Slub | *"I am within tolerance."* / *"I am reporting myself."* / *"There is no one to report to."* | an irregularity it can't report |
| Descant | *"He's flat again."* / *"We go with him."* / *"It's easier than telling him."* | someone else's mistake |
| Footnote | *"Down here."* / *"You've read past me."* / *"That's alright. Most do."* | a readership |

## 7.2.4 Resolution Scenes — 168

Every enemy type has a bespoke SEVER scene and a bespoke UNKNOT scene. **No fade-outs.**

**UNKNOT scenes average 20 seconds.** They are short, specific, and never triumphant.

> **FILING ERROR — UNKNOT.** Aven writes it a category on a blank card. It reads the card.
> It files *itself*, correctly, into a drawer it selects, and closes the drawer from the
> inside. **It is the first thing in the game to thank the player.**

> **FILING ERROR — SEVER.** The cards fall. They stay fallen. If Osk is present, he picks
> them up, and does not comment, and the player watches him do it.

> **COOLING-OFF — SEVER.** It does not resist. It was already leaving. The scene is four
> seconds long and there is no sound.

Lines generated by systems rather than scenes. **All are authored per character; none are

## 7.2.6 The Three Questions

The Journal's *Questions* tab ends the game with exactly three open on a completionist run
(achievement #52). They are:

1. **"Who was the second keepsake in the double grave?"** — closable only via Q08, which
   fails if Moth is unwoven.
2. **"Why did the drawer say RESERVED before anyone reserved it?"** — the date on Aven's
   card is Year 6, and Aven was born ~Year 880. **This is never explained.** Merit filed
   an entry for a person who did not exist yet, and the game offers no mechanism for it,
   and the Assayer, asked directly, says: *"It is correctly filed."*
3. **"What was the Cantor's name?"** — spoken once, on a Keeping inscription, in a room
   most players reach after the Cantor is resolved.

**These three stay open on every route, every ending, and into NG+.** They are the
deliberate residue, and closing them would be a mistake.

## 7.2.7 Localisation Notes

Flagged now, per GDD §15.7:

| Problem | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| **"Ward"** carries guarded-thing / hospital-ward / warding-off in English | Loc leads choose a term per language **before Stage 7 drafting completes.** Non-negotiable dependency. |
| **Moth's quotations** | Compiler emits a manifest linking each Moth line to its source line ID. Translators must render Moth as a quotation of *that language's* version of the original, not a fresh translation. |
| **The five tones** | Must survive as distinguishable *registers*, not just word choice. Loc receives the tone histogram per character. |
| **`[INTERRUPT]` truncation** | Languages with different word order need a per-language cut point. The compiler exposes it as a per-line field rather than assuming the em-dash. |
| **Marrow-Pot's name gag** | Untranslatable as a pun. Loc is instructed to **replace, not translate** — the requirement is "a name he refuses to explain for four hours that turns out to be a person," not the specific joke. |
| **Text expansion** | +35% budget on all UI layouts (DE/RU worst case). |
