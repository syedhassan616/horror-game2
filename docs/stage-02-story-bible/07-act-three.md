# 07 — Act III: Three Routes

**Runtime:** ~90 minutes · **Scenes:** S-301 → S-372
**Question the act answers:** *Who asked for this?*
**Answer it delivers:** *Three people, all of whom were right.*

---

## 7.1 Route Architecture

Act III is **a different set of regions per route** (GDD §06.7). This is the largest
single content cost in the project and the primary replay driver.

| Route | Gate (Act II checkpoint) | Regions | Petition surfaced | Companion state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CARRY** | Mercy high, Wrath low, Hope ≥ 55 | Orrery → Commonplace → **Ash Garden** | **Rue's** (the personal petition) | Moth authored; Vellum defects |
| **LEDGER** | Wrath high | Orrery → **The Rendering** → Ash Garden | **Annike's** (the bulk petition) | Moth silent; Quill replaces Vellum |
| **DRIFT** | Mixed / low both | Commonplace → **The Undersleep** → Orrery | **Merit's own** (the original petition) | Moth unwoven; Vellum promoted |

**Shared scenes:** the Orrery, the Commonplace's core, and the Assay entrance are common
to all three, written once, with route-conditional dialogue. Everything else is exclusive.

**The completionist truth** — available only to a player who has run all three, and never
stated by any character: **no single petition caused the Ninth Quiet.** The machine
assembled a rule from three reasonable requests filed eleven weeks apart (§10).

## 7.2 Shared: The Orrery

### S-301 ATTACHMENT [MANDATORY]
Traversal verb: re-parent an island's gravity. Islands with strong bonds orbit tightly.
Islands with none drift. **The overworld version of the tether**, and the first time the
player uses the core metaphor to walk somewhere.

The region has **no weather**, after seven regions that did. The stillness is a shock and
nobody comments on it.

### S-302 ASTRONOMER VEY [MANDATORY — Quest 32]
Charts bonds as orbits. Her chart has one orbit around nothing.

> **VEY.** "It's not an error. I've checked it for nine years. Something is holding
> position around a point where there is demonstrably nothing, and it has been doing so
> since before the chart existed."
> **AVEN** [CURIOUS] "What was there?"
> **VEY.** "That's the wrong question and I love you for it. The question is what's
> *still there*, and the answer is: whatever is doing the holding."

**F12 setup.** The point has a catalogue name. It is **Corrin Vane**.

### S-303 THE UNTETHERED [OPTIONAL]
A person who drifted loose and likes it. The game's only cheerful argument for
severance that isn't institutional, and it is genuinely persuasive:

> **THE UNTETHERED.** "Everyone assumes I'm sad. I'm having the best decade of my life.
> I've been to nine places. I don't check the sky for anybody." *(beat)* "Ask me if I'd
> go back."
> **AVEN** [BLUNT] "Would you?"
> **THE UNTETHERED.** *(long pause)* "...I'd like the question to stop being interesting."

### S-304 THE ORRERY'S KEEPER [MANDATORY — BOSS B7]
Three islands, independent gravity, fourth phase upside down.

## 7.3 Shared: The Commonplace

### S-311 EDDA GRIST [MANDATORY]
Blind. Reads by touching emotional weight. Has read everything.

> **EDDA.** "You want me to tell you it means something." *(she does not look up, because
> she does not look)* "I catalogue. I don't appraise."
> **AVEN** [WRY] "That's a very convenient philosophy for someone who's read everything."
> **EDDA.** *(the first time she smiles)* "Yes. It is."

She grants **READ**. It becomes the diagnostic tool for UNKNOT and the key to nine
puzzles and the entire superboss chain.

### S-312 THE MEMORY PUZZLE [MANDATORY — the flagship]
Edda asks Aven what Osk said in the first ninety seconds of the game.

Four options. The game logged it (S-004). It was never highlighted. There is no penalty
for being wrong and no reward for being right, **except that Edda looks at you
differently and so does the player at themselves.**

The correct answer is: *"It hurts in the mornings and I've got nothing else of hers."*

### S-313 THE PAGE SHE WON'T TOUCH [MANDATORY — Quest 33, ★, FAILABLE] ★ **F11**
One page. She will not say why.

It is **Merit Vane's own severance request, in Merit's hand.** Forcing her to read it —
possible, and the game permits it without comment — changes her permanently and reduces
her final scene to two lines.

**What it says** (visible only if read):
> *"Subject: self. Reason: I have built a thing that requires a person who cannot be
> moved by the people it will hurt, and I am the only applicant. Countersignature:
> none available. Note: I would like it recorded that I asked twice whether there was
> another way. There was not, by my own analysis, which I no longer entirely trust."*

### S-314 NINE DOORS [OPTIONAL — Quest 35, ★]
The superboss chain's cross-reference step. The Journal becomes a search tool.

## 7.4 CARRY Route — The Ash Garden

### S-321 NOBODY FOUGHT HERE [MANDATORY]
Two armies met. Neither wanted to watch the other die. Both severed, en masse, in an
afternoon. Everyone walked home.

**There are no weapons here.** Somebody collected them and stacked them neatly and went
home. The stacks are still there, rusted into shape.

### S-322 THE GARDENER [MANDATORY]
He was in the battle. He remembers walking home. He has been here since.

> **THE GARDENER.** "I remember the walk. Four days. Lovely weather." *(beat)* "I remember
> being *relieved*, and I've had two hundred years to work out what I was relieved about,
> and I've got nowhere."

### S-323 THE NAMED FLOWERS [MANDATORY] ★ **F12 PAYOFF**
Each flower carries the name of a person **the player** has lost this save file —
procedurally assembled from the actual playthrough. Osk. Ossa, if she was severed. Coom.
Anyone who died.

**And one you don't recognise: CORRIN VANE.**

Aven does not know the name. No NPC explains it. The Journal logs it under *Questions*
and it stays there.

### S-324 FERRIS [OPTIONAL — Quest 34, FAILABLE]
A boy picking flowers by the armful. He is picking the ones with his village's names on,
so nobody else has to come here and see them.

### S-325 RUE'S PETITION [MANDATORY — Choice C] ★ **THE ACT III TWIST (CARRY)**
The form is here, in the Ash Garden's Registry cairn, because Rue filed it at Hushfell
and Hushfell files to the cairn.

**Form 12-B. On behalf of another: ☒. Relationship to subject(s): Captain.**

Rue is standing behind Aven when they read it. She does not pretend.

> **RUE.** "I filed it in the autumn."
> **AVEN** [BLUNT] "You did this."
> **RUE.** "I asked for one thing. I asked that when I go, my crew doesn't stop." *(beat)*
> "Four hundred people at Alder Stand, and every one of them fine, and I have watched
> what a crew does when the captain — "
> **RUE.** *(and this is the only time she loses the thread)* " — I did the arithmetic
> and I got a number and the number was worth it."
> **AVEN** [CAREFUL] "What number?"
> **RUE.** "Eleven. Eleven people I'd have taken with me." *(beat)* "It's given them
> everybody. I know."

**She found out in Act II and kept moving.** That is who she is. It is not a defence and
she does not offer it as one.

**Choice C's three outcomes** — GDD §06.5.C, all authored in full.

### S-326 BOTH ARMIES [OPTIONAL — BOSS B9]
A boss with no aggression. It is walking home. Stopping it is the fight, and there is no
violent solution, and the game gives you every violent tool.

**UNKNOT:** you say the one thing that makes an army turn around. The answer is in the
flower names — you read them **Corrin Vane's**, the first severance, the name none of them
knew, and two hundred years of "we did the merciful thing" acquires a first entry.

### S-327 VELLUM DEFECTS [ROUTE: CARRY]
Fourth encounter. He has grown a Ward — **for Aven** — and he itemises every mercy Aven
has shown him, on a form, out loud, because it is the only register he has.

> **VELLUM.** "Item four. At Verrick, second audit, you did not pursue when I withdrew,
> and I have been unable to file that." *(beat)* "There isn't a box."

## 7.5 LEDGER Route — The Rendering

### S-341 THE RENDERING [ROUTE: LEDGER — exclusive region]
Where the Assayer processes at scale. Not a factory of horrors — an **intake hall**,
clean, well-lit, staffed by people on shifts who have lanyards and lunch breaks.

It is the most disturbing region in the game because **it is well-run**.

### S-342 THE SHIFT [MANDATORY]
Aven can work a shift. The game lets you. It is not a trap and there is no punishment.
It is four minutes of correctly performed clerical work, and it is easy, and you are good
at it.

### S-343 ANNIKE'S PETITION [ROUTE: LEDGER] ★ **THE ACT III TWIST (LEDGER)**
The bulk petition. Three hundred willing candidates, filed as one body, under the Fourth
Quiet's precedent (§02.2).

**Annike is here.** She came when she heard. She is not defensive and she does not
crumble.

> **ANNIKE.** "Three hundred people asked me. I filed three hundred requests as one
> document because the Registry takes nine weeks per individual and people were dying
> in the queue." *(beat)* "That is the entire crime."
> **AVEN** [BLUNT] "It used you as a precedent for everyone."
> **ANNIKE.** "Yes." *(and she does not say *I didn't know*, because she is not going to
> hide behind it)* "I would do it again for the three hundred. I have no idea what to do
> about the rest, and I have been standing here for two days."

### S-344 VELLUM, FINAL AUDIT [ROUTE: LEDGER]
He does not defect. He is competent, and sad, and he does his job, and the fight is
straightforward and awful. **Quill takes over afterwards**, if the player pushed him
(Quest 22), wearing the lanyard and using Vellum's cadence.

## 7.6 DRIFT Route — The Undersleep

### S-361 THE TWELFTH CAR [ROUTE: DRIFT — Quest 28, ★]
Rue's key. What is in the car is not cargo: it is **her mother's house, dismantled and
reassembled** — House Twelve from Wyndmarrow (F03), moved piece by piece over sixty
years by a woman who cannot stop moving and cannot leave it behind either.

There are no photographs. There never were.

### S-362 THE UNDERSLEEP [ROUTE: DRIFT — exclusive region]
Sleep in the twelfth car. A soft, wrong version of everywhere, populated by people **as
Aven remembers them** rather than as they are.

Osk is here, unsevered, mid-sentence, forever. He finishes the sentence. He never gets
further.

**This is the only unambiguously warm place in the game and it is a lie**, and the game
never says so.

### S-363 MERIT'S PETITION [ROUTE: DRIFT] ★ **THE ACT III TWIST (DRIFT)**
The original. Year 6. Merit filed on themselves, establishing the precedent that a
petitioner may act on a subject who cannot meaningfully consent — because Merit, having
already been severed once in intent, argued in the document that they no longer could.

**It is a legal manoeuvre performed by a grieving engineer to make themselves eligible
for their own machine**, and every subsequent precedent descends from it.

### S-364 THE VERSION OF YOU THAT DIDN'T GO [ROUTE: DRIFT — SECRET BOSS S1]
An Aven who stayed in the drawer. Fights with the player's own build and mirrors the last
30 seconds of their inputs. Beatable only by playing differently than you have played all
game.

> **THE VERSION.** *(in Aven's own text box, same font, no tone tag)* "You could have
> stayed. Nobody would have known. Nobody was looking for you."
> **THE VERSION.** "I'm not a threat. I'm an option. That's worse."

## 7.7 Shared: The Approach

### S-371 THE FUNERAL [MANDATORY — all routes] ★ **THE RUNNING GAG PAYS OFF**
Whoever the player has lost — and the game guarantees at least one — is buried at
Wyndmarrow. The bell-ringing competition, established in Act I as pure village comedy,
pays off here **once, hard** (GDD §01.5.6).

Guisley, if alive, rings. If dead, the frame hangs empty and nobody fills it, and the
count is read one short, and Tilly — if she is alive and unsevered — does not correct it.

**That is her arc's turn.** She lets the count be wrong.

### S-372 THE STAIR UP [MANDATORY]
The ascent to the Assay. Whoever remains walks it with Aven. If nobody remains, Aven
walks it alone and the game does not comment, and the tether renders with only the faint
second line — Merit's, in a colour no companion produces, which has been there since the
Prologue (F02).
