# 05 — Act I: The Ringing

**Runtime:** ~75 minutes · **Scenes:** S-101 → S-148 · **Regions:** Wyndmarrow,
The Grieving Wood, Hushfell
**Question the act answers:** *Who is doing this?*
**Answer it delivers:** *Nobody. It was asked for.*

---

## 5.1 Act Structure

| Movement | Region | Scenes | Function |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I-A | Wyndmarrow | S-101–118 | The Bell Count. Tilly. The village pretending. |
| I-B | The Grieving Wood | S-119–131 | Moth. Environmental storytelling as language. |
| I-C | Hushfell | S-132–142 | Annike. **The first choice that cannot be made well.** |
| I-D | Wyndmarrow, return | S-143–148 | The Tally. The act-one twist. |

**Order:** Wyndmarrow → Wood → Hushfell is enforced; the Wood and Hushfell can be
partially interleaved. The Tally fires on return to Wyndmarrow with `flag:annike_resolved`.

## 5.2 Wyndmarrow

### S-101 ELEVEN SILENCES [MANDATORY]
Aven arrives at dawn. The village is ringing. It sounds wonderful.

The tower board — a great slate with 90 chalk marks — is being updated by a boy with a
ladder. He rubs out eleven. Nobody watching says anything. Somebody starts a conversation
about the weather **at volume**, and three people join it gratefully.

**This is the region's whole character in one shot: a community performing normality at
each other, competently, out of love.**

`world:bell_count = 79`

### S-102 THE ELEVENTH BELL [MANDATORY]
```
Cast   Aven, Tilly Brack
```
A girl of eleven is ringing two bells. She has worked out a rope arrangement to do it and
the arrangement is *good* — she is genuinely a competent engineer and nobody has noticed.

> **TILLY.** "Don't help. If you help it's two people and one bell, and that's not what
> it's for."
> **AVEN** [CAREFUL] "What's it for?"
> **TILLY.** "The count." *(beat)* "The count has to be right."

She does not explain further. She has no interest in Aven. She is busy.

**The recruitment is not a recruitment.** Tilly attaches herself to Aven three scenes
later, when Aven turns out to be able to hear the difference between a rung bell and a
rung-for bell (Aven has no Bearing and therefore listens instead — §1.6 paying off in
under ten minutes of screen time).

### S-103 PELL'S PROVISIONS [MANDATORY]
Marrow-Warden Pell runs the shop and the Registry post. She is brisk, kind, and stocks
seventeen items. Over the game she will stock fewer. She never explains why.

**She is where the requisition slip fails.** Pell reads 12-B/1441, goes still, and
files it — and tells Aven, honestly, that she has no idea what it is, that she has been
in this job thirty years, and that the number is nine hundred years old.

> **PELL.** "That's not a live number. That's a — " *(she checks something)* — "that's a
> *reserved* number. From the founding." *(beat)* "Who gave you this?"
> **AVEN** [BLUNT] "A man who doesn't remember giving it to me."
> **PELL.** "...Right. Well. That's the week I'm having."

### S-104 WHAT HALDEN KEPT [MANDATORY — Quest 02]
Tilly's father. Severed six weeks ago. He is sorting his wife Odile's belongings into
keep/discard with perfect logic and no criteria, because he cannot generate criteria.

He asks Aven to decide for him. **He is not distressed. He is stuck**, the way you get
stuck on a form.

> **HALDEN.** "This is a cup. She used it. I know she used it because there's a chip and
> I remember the chip." *(pause)* "So it's — is that a keep? What makes a keep?"
> **AVEN** [WARM] "Does it matter to you?"
> **HALDEN.** "That's what I'm asking you. I can't get at it."

The quest is: Aven sorts eleven objects. Each choice is logged. **In the epilogue, if
Odile is returned, Halden goes looking for the things Aven discarded**, and the game
tells you exactly what you threw away.

### S-105 ODILE [MANDATORY]
Odile Brack is severed, at home, cooking. She is warm to Tilly the way you are warm to a
neighbour's child. She has never once been unkind and never will be.

**The staging is the horror:** she calls Tilly "sweetheart," gets her name right, asks
about her day, and listens to the answer. Everything is correct and nothing lands.

Tilly does not react. Tilly has had six weeks of practice.

> **ODILE.** "Sweetheart, you've been up since four again."
> **TILLY.** "I've got the rota."
> **ODILE.** "You have. You're very good." *(to Aven, warmly)* "She's very good. Is she
> yours?"
> **TILLY.** *(already leaving)* "Come on."

### S-106–110 THE VILLAGE [OPTIONAL — 5 scenes]
Guisley the bell-founder (Quest 03, superboss chain). The rota dispute (Quest 05, comedy
that turns). House Twelve (Quest 04, **F03**). Nine named NPCs, three conversation stages
each. The bell-ringing competition established as the region's running gag.

**The gag's rule (GDD §01.5.6):** it pays off *once*, hard, at a funeral, in Act III.

### S-111 THE THIRD BELL [MANDATORY]
Aven hears the difference. Tilly notices Aven noticing. **This is the recruitment.**

> **TILLY.** "You heard that."
> **AVEN** [QUIET] "..."
> **TILLY.** "You *heard* it. Nobody hears it. It's the third bell, it's — the note's the
> same but it's *early*, because I'm reaching, because it's not my rope."
> **TILLY.** *(this is the most important thing anyone has ever said to her)* "You heard
> it."

She seats. **First tether: ANCHOR.** The tutorial for PLANT happens on the walk out of
the village, against Bellwork enemies, with Tilly narrating rope theory.

### S-112–118 [MANDATORY + OPTIONAL]
Quests 01, 04, 05 available. First Bellwork encounters. **F02 payoff setup:** in the
first fight with Tilly seated, the tether renders in Tilly's colour — but a *second*,
fainter line persists underneath it in a colour no companion produces. It is visible in
every fight in the game. It is never mentioned.

## 5.3 The Grieving Wood

### S-119 THE SEXTON [MANDATORY]
A man burying keepsakes professionally, humming. Forty in his backlog. He has stopped.
He will not say why for two more visits (Quest 06): he buried his own, and cannot start
again, and the humming is what he does instead of the sentence.

### S-120 ROOT READING [MANDATORY — mechanic]
Dig at a tree, learn the bond. Twenty readable trees. Three plot-critical.

The teaching tree is a mundane one and the game does not flag it:
> *A tin whistle. Buried Year 899 by a woman for her brother, who was not dead, and who
> had asked her to stop writing.*

### S-121 MOTH [MANDATORY]
It is standing slightly too close before the player notices it is there.

> **MOTH.** *(in Osk's voice, exactly)* "— you're not stock. You're *in* stock."
> **AVEN** [any] *(portrait: 7, Startled)*
> **MOTH.** *(in Pell's voice)* "Who gave you this?"
> **MOTH.** *(in a woman's voice the player has not heard)* "I'll be an hour."

**That third quote is Corrin Vane's, in Nell's memory of it, and it is spoken 3 minutes
into the region, six hours before it means anything.**

Moth is not threatening. Moth is *interested*. It follows. It quotes. It is very good
company and deeply upsetting for about twenty minutes, after which most players adopt it
completely.

**F04:** within the region, Moth quotes a line in Rue Alderman's voice. Aven has not met
Rue. There is no way to know. Players who replay find it in the first five minutes.

### S-122 WHAT MOTH IS [MANDATORY]
The Sexton explains, because the Sexton is the only one who talks to Moth normally.

> **SEXTON.** "Forty of them. Unclaimed, all of them, found each other down in the roots
> and — " *(shrugs)* — "well. Something has to happen when forty things want the same
> thing and nobody's using them."
> **SEXTON.** "It counted itself once. Told me the number. Didn't like it much. Doesn't
> like being asked."

**Moth seats. Second tether: GHOST.**

### S-123–131 [Quests 06–09, Briarsome, 20 trees]
**S-127 — TWO TREES, ONE ROOT** (Quest 07) is the act's best optional scene and the
first time the game asks a question it doesn't answer: two people who died not speaking
have grown together underground. Aven can separate them or leave them. Neither consented
to either.

**S-129 — BRIARSOME** [OPTIONAL BOSS]. Arena shrinks permanently. Under it: a child's
shoe. The UNKNOT requires digging mid-fight.

**S-131 — SOMETHING BURIED TWICE** (Quest 08, ★, FAILABLE): a grave with two keepsakes,
one recent. It is one of Moth's forty. If the player has already unwoven Moth — impossible
in Act I, possible on replay — the quest cannot resolve and the Journal question stays
open permanently.

## 5.4 Hushfell

### S-132 THE KIND ROOM [MANDATORY]
The approach is white and silent. The monastery interior is **warm, busy, and smells of
bread** — the region's forbidden colour (warm yellow) appears exactly once, in the kitchen
window seen from outside, and the art direction is doing an argument.

### S-133 SISTER ANNIKE [MANDATORY]
She is making tea when Aven arrives and does not stop. She explains the rite completely,
unprompted, without defensiveness, in about ninety seconds — because she has explained it
several thousand times and has stopped finding any of it difficult.

> **ANNIKE.** "Three days. Full disclosure, which means I sit down and I tell them
> precisely what they will and won't have afterwards, and I don't soften it, because the
> softening is what makes it a lie."
> **ANNIKE.** "They can leave at any point in the three days. Forty-one per cent do."
> **AVEN** [BLUNT] "And you do it yourself."
> **ANNIKE.** "I do it myself." *(she pours)* "Would you have someone else do it?"

**Writing rule for Annike:** she never argues. She *answers*. Every attack Aven makes,
she has heard, and she gives it its due, and she is still standing there having done it
three hundred times.

### S-134 THE REGISTER OF WILLING [OPTIONAL — Quest 13, ★]
300 entries. All short. All reasonable. Reading all 300 is achievement #45 and changes
Annike's final scene.

Sample entries, and the standard:
> *#7. Farrier. Carried his apprentice, who drowned. Reason given: "I can't shoe a horse
> and I have to shoe horses."*
> *#88. No occupation listed. Reason given: "I would like to be able to visit my sister."*
> *#211. Registry clerk, 61. Reason given: "I have read the forms for thirty years and I
> would like to stop understanding them."*
> *#290. Reason given: "None." Entry accepted. Note in Annike's hand: "Asked four times.
> Answer did not change. Accepted."*

### S-135 CANDIDATE OSSA [MANDATORY — Quest 10, FAILABLE]
Third day of three. Her son died in the spring. She wants to talk about anything else,
and Aven can let her, or not.

**She is not fragile.** She is funny, and specific, and she has thought about this more
carefully than Aven has.

> **OSSA.** "Don't. I've had the speech from Fen twice and he cries. I'd rather talk
> about your hair, which is a *situation*."
> **AVEN** [WRY] "It's been a week."
> **OSSA.** "It's been a week for both of us."

**If the player argues her out of it**, she thanks them, and in Act III she is still
feeling it, and she thanks them again, and she is not okay, and she does not blame them.
**If the third day passes**, she is severed, and well, and asks Aven if they're new here.

### S-136 BROTHER FEN [OPTIONAL — Quest 11, ⇄ with Ossa]
He has withdrawn four times. Today is the fifth. He is the comic relief of Hushfell and
the reason the region isn't unbearable, and his fifth withdrawal — or not — is mutually
exclusive with Ossa's outcome, because Annike can only take one candidate through in the
window Aven is present.

**Neither the player nor any character is told this trade-off exists.** It is discovered
by comparing playthroughs.

### S-137 THE FORM [MANDATORY] ★ **F05**
Aven can read a blank 12-B. Section 4:

> *ON BEHALF OF ANOTHER? ☐   Relationship to subject(s): ________*

Aven can ask about it. Annike answers plainly, and the answer is the entire plot:

> **ANNIKE.** "Rarely. It needs the subject's countersignature, or a finding that the
> subject can't give one." *(beat)* "It gets used for the dying, mostly. Someone who
> wants to spare the people who'll be left."
> **ANNIKE.** "I've processed four in nineteen years."
> **AVEN** [CAREFUL] "Four."
> **ANNIKE.** "Four." *(and she moves on, because it is not, to her, an interesting number)*

**One of the four is Rue's.** Annike does not remember it particularly. It was correctly
filed.

### S-138 CHOICE A — THE RITE [MANDATORY — GDD §06.5]
Shut it down / endorse it / walk away. All three permanently reshape Hushfell, Annike's
arc, Ossa, Fen, and three Act III scenes.

**Writing rule:** no option is presented as brave. Aven's lines for all three are flat.

### S-139–142 [Quest 12, the Three-Day Wait, Annike's optional fight]
**S-141 — ANNIKE, IF PUSHED** [OPTIONAL BOSS]. She never attacks. She parries and talks.
Killing her is trivial and permanently closes the True Ending, and **nothing warns the
player**. The Register gains a 301st entry in her hand, dated that day, reason given:
*"I have been asked a question I can't file."*

## 5.5 The Act I Twist

### S-143 THE TALLY ARRIVES [MANDATORY]
Return to Wyndmarrow. The Bell Count has dropped again while Aven was away — the world
moved without the player, which is the first time the game does this and it does it
without comment.

The Tally is in the tower, counting. It is not attacking anyone. It is *auditing*, and
the audit is lethal because it counts Aven too.

### S-144 THE COUNT [MANDATORY — BOSS B1]
Mechanics in GDD §08.2. Narratively: the fight is a bureaucratic procedure that the
player must interrupt by demonstrating a category error.

**UNKNOT:** Aven shows it a **rung-for bell** — Tilly's arrangement, ringing Odile's bell.
The Assayer's rule set has no provision for a person performing another person's
continued regard (§1.8). The Tally excuses itself. Politely. It files a query.

> **THE TALLY.** "Query. Bell 44 rung. Bearer 44 severed Year 903 week 2. Bell 44 rung."
> **THE TALLY.** "Rung by whom."
> **TILLY.** "Me."
> **THE TALLY.** *(a long pause; the metronome continues)* "That is not a category."

### S-145 THE QUERY [MANDATORY] ★ **THE ACT I TWIST**
The Tally, before it leaves, states its authority. It is the first time the machine's
nature is visible:

> **THE TALLY.** "Audit conducted under standing directive. Directive amended Year 902,
> autumn, on receipt of petition."
> **AVEN** [BLUNT] "Whose petition?"
> **THE TALLY.** "Petitions are confidential to the petitioner."
> **AVEN** [BLUNT] "*Whose.*"
> **THE TALLY.** "The requisition is available at any Registry post. The petitioner is
> not." *(beat)* "You have one. You are holding it."

**Aven's requisition slip — Osk's beautiful, proud, accidental slip — is a live thread
into the Assayer's intake system.** Requisition 12-B/1441.

**The act's answer:** the Assayer is not malfunctioning, not evil, and not hunting.
It is **following instructions somebody gave it.**

### S-146 WHAT PELL SAYS [MANDATORY]
Pell reads the amended directive. It takes her a while. She is not a coward and she does
not soften it.

> **PELL.** "Right. So. Somebody filed a 12-B in the autumn, and the machine's — "
> *(she reads it again)* — "it's *generalised* off it. That's not a word they use. That's
> the word for what it's done."
> **PELL.** "Whoever they are, they asked for one person. It's given them everyone."

### S-147 TILLY DECIDES [MANDATORY]
Tilly comes. This is not negotiable and Aven does not get a choice about it.

> **TILLY.** "I'm coming."
> **AVEN** [WARM] "Your dad — "
> **TILLY.** "Is fine. That's the whole thing. He's *fine.*" *(beat, and this is the
> first crack)* "Everyone here is fine."

### S-148 THE NORTH ROAD [MANDATORY]
Exit to Act II. The requisition number is the objective. The Lamplight March is the
destination, because a requisition trail needs a Registry that moves faster than the
audit does.

**Act I exit state:** `bell_count` · `flag:annike_*` · `flag:ossa_*` · `flag:moth_seated`
· `flag:tilly_joined` · Eight values first checkpoint · **route not yet determined**
