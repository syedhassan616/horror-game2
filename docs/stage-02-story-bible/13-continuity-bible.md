# 13 — Continuity Bible

**The rules that keep 96,000 words consistent across three routes, five endings, and
eleven writers.** Anything here overrides a writer's preference. Anything not here is a
judgement call — make it, then add it here.

## 13.2 Terminology Discipline

| Use | Not | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ward** | soul, bond-heart, second soul | It is an organ. Physical. |
| **Severance / severed** | erasure, wiping, the cut | The Registry's word; institutional |
| **Hollow** | — | Slang, offensive, **children only**. Never in narration or by a sympathetic adult. |
| **The Bearing** | the sense, the link | |
| **Unmoored** | grieving, bereaved | Vesselmere has no neutral word for bereaved and that absence is worldbuilding |
| **Carrying threshold** | falling in love | Never romantic by default |
| **Petition / directive / precedent** | order, command, decree | Bureaucratic register throughout |
| **Filed / transferred / holding** | destroyed, deleted | The Assayer's vocabulary is never violent |

**The Assayer's register:** it uses only intake vocabulary. It never says *kill, take,
remove, hurt, help, save, mercy,* or *sorry.* Its cruellest lines are its most procedural.

## 13.3 The Hard Facts (do not contradict)

1. Severance destroys **significance**, never memory. §1.2.
2. Severance is **irreversible** except by physical return from the Keeping, within
   half-life. No love, reunion, willpower, or narrative reverses it. **Zero exceptions.**
3. A person carries **one** Ward. Broad-bearers (two) are 1-in-10,000 and Aven is *not*
   one.
4. Aven has **no Bearing**, cannot be severed, and cannot grow a Ward of their own.
5. The Assayer has **never lied** and **cannot act without a petition or derived
   precedent.**
6. 61,400 Wards are in the Keeping. The plate is correct.
7. Storage half-life is **41 days**. Nine-century returns are partial.
8. Grief-stop killed **90,000** after the Rot. Verifiable. True.
9. The current year is **903**. The Ninth Quiet began winter 903.
10. Three petitions, all real, all true on every route. §10.
11. **Nobody in this story does the wrong thing for the wrong reason.** Bureaucratic
    momentum is the only villain.

## 13.5 Scene Review Checklist

Every scene must pass all nine or it goes back:

1. Does every character in it **want something**, including the ones passing through?
2. Is there a line that only **this** character could say?
3. Does it advance **character, lore, or a future event**? (Two of three preferred.)
4. Is any lore delivered as **somebody's problem** rather than as information?
5. Does anyone **state a theme**? → cut it.
6. Does the **camera or music** tell the player how to feel? → remove it.
7. If it's funny, does the comedy **carry weight** rather than relieve it?
8. Does at least one NPC **remember** something specific from earlier?
9. Could a QA tester describe **what the player did** by walking this room with dialogue
   disabled?

## 13.6 Route & State Discipline

- **Never write a line that assumes a companion is present.** Every scene must read with
  the roster reduced to zero. QA runs an "alone pass" on every scene.
- **Never write a line that assumes an NPC is alive.** Eleven can die, nine can be
  severed. Every scene referencing them needs a `severed` and an `absent` variant.
- **Never reference a region the route may not have visited.** Act II is order-free.
- **Never let a severed character express a preference, a longing, or a regret.** They
  can express *facts*, *habits*, and *courtesy*. This is the single most-violated rule in
  drafts and it destroys the premise every time.
- **Never resolve a Journal *Question* automatically.** The player closes them or they
  stay open. Three are designed to stay open forever.
