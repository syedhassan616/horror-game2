# 11 — Foreshadowing & Payoff Ledger

**QA contract:** every plant must be *reachable before* its payoff on **every route**.
This ledger is the checklist. CI's Route Prover (GDD §14.6) asserts plant-before-payoff
ordering on all three routes and fails the build on a violation.

## 11.4 Anti-Foreshadowing (deliberate dead ends)

Players theorise. These exist to be *wrong*, and each is resolved explicitly so the
theory dies rather than lingering:

| Red herring | Why players will bite | How it's closed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aven is Merit reincarnated | The reserved drawer, the chair fitting | S-406 states plainly: Aven is a person who was put in a drawer. §1.9. |
| Moth is Corrin | It quotes Nell | Moth is forty unclaimed Wards, none of them his. Stated by the Sexton, S-122. |
| Tilly will die | She is eleven and beloved | She can be severed, not killed. Her death is not authored in any route. |
| The Assayer is lying about the 90,000 | It's a villain | The figures are verifiable in the Salt Ledger, S-212. It has never lied. |
| The Keeping is a trap / the Wards are gone | Too good to be true | They are there. All 61,400. The plate on the wall is correct. |
| There is a way to save everyone | RPG convention | There is not. §1.9. Three endings are good and all three cost something. |

**Rule:** every red herring must be *closed on screen*, not left ambiguous. Ambiguity is
reserved for the moral questions, never the factual ones.
