## VERRICK *(continued)*

### Q19 · BARRO'S EYELETS ⚠ — **Choice B**
```
Giver Barro · VRK-13 · Act II · Failable · Reveals both
```
Three years of night shifts, finished. Eleven regulation eyelets, hand-set.
**Does he ask Dov if he wants it?**

**Outcomes.**
- **Ask.** Dov says no, kindly, and is right. Barro burns it. `flag:barro_asked` — his
  epilogue module variant A; he and Dov stay married.
- **Install it without asking.** It works. Dov returns and is **furious about the consent,
  not the crime.** `flag:dov_restored`, `flag:dov_angry`. Their marriage may not survive.
  Wrath +, Hope +.
- **Turn him in** *(only available at Trust < 30)*. Barro is executed. Floor Nine runs a
  shift short and nobody mentions it. Idrisse never speaks to Aven again but does not stop
  them working there, which is worse.
- **If Q18 was told to Barro:** a fourth branch — he can source fresh stock, and the weave
  would actually last, and the question becomes harder rather than easier.

### Q20 · WHAT IDRISSE DOESN'T SAY
Barro's boss knows and reports nothing. Complicity as an act of love. Her entire answer is
*"Floor Nine hits quota."* Pushing her three times yields one extra sentence and costs
Trust.

### Q21 · THE COMPLAINTS BOX
Bolted shut, full, never opened. Two hundred complaints, none about work.
**Outcomes.** Read them all *(Keepsake ×2, achievement #56, and a permanent change to how
five Verrick NPCs greet Aven)* / hand them to Idrisse *(she reads them; the box is
unbolted; nothing else changes and that is the point)*.

### Q22 · QUILL'S PROMOTION ⚠⇄
Encourage the auditor's apprentice into the job or out of it. **The pipeline that makes
Vellums.**
**Outcomes.** In *(Act III: Quill with a lanyard and Vellum's exact cadence — and if
Vellum defects, Quill replaces him)* / out *(he goes home to Verrick; his mother is
delighted; he is unemployed and fine)* / **say nothing** *(he decides alone, and the coin
lands on `in`, and the game does not tell the player it was weighted)*.

## THE DROWNED CHOIR

### Q23 · SENNET'S SURFACE ⚠⇄
He wants to leave and cannot say so in front of the choir.
**Hard rule: fails if ≥10 voices have been silenced.** He was never going to leave a
smaller choir. **The game never states this**; it is found by comparison.
**Outcomes.** He surfaces *(companion; CURRENT unlocked)* / he stays *(he is fine; the
fourth part is held; Aven never gets CURRENT this run)*.

### Q24 · TEACHING THE CHORISTER
A severed singer sings tunelessly. Nobody has told him. Do you?
**Outcomes.** Tell him *(he thanks Aven, adjusts, sings correctly and joylessly forever)* /
don't *(forty people continue arranging around one wrong note, every day, out of love, and
you can hear them doing it for the rest of the game)*.
**The game refuses to prefer one.** No Eight values move.

### Q25 · THE PART THAT ISN'T SUNG ★
Forty-one seats, forty singers, one scored rest. **Superboss chain steps 2–3.** Collecting
all 41 parts and playing them in *seat order* (not collection order) in MAR-08 speaks a
name.

## LAMPLIGHT MARCH

### Q26 · OTTOLINE'S LETTER ⚠
She has written to Rue and can't deliver it (full text in Stage 4 §4.6).
**Outcomes.** Deliver *(Rue thanks her and reassigns her to the front car — kindness as
exile)* / read and return it *(Ottoline is humiliated and grateful)* / **burn it**
*(requires Trust ≥ 60; Ottoline never knows; she asks Aven once, in Act III, whether they
did, and Aven can lie)*.

### Q27 · WHY WE DON'T STOP
Alder Stand: four hundred people, nine days, all fine at the end of it. Told flatly, as
operational history. **Establishes the Eighth Quiet's precedent** and Rue's entire
psychology in one conversation.

### Q28 · THE TWELFTH CAR ★
The sealed car. Rue's key.
**Inside: House Twelve, dismantled and reassembled** — moved piece by piece over sixty
years by a woman who cannot stop moving and cannot leave it behind.
**Opens the Undersleep.** Rue does not stop Aven and does not come in.

### Q29 · COOK'S NAME
Marrow-Pot refuses to explain his name for four hours across six conversations. The sixth
lands in Act III: **the pot is named after a person.** His mother's cook, who fed a
settlement through the Eighth Quiet and whose name nobody wrote down, so it went on the pot.

### Q30 · THE SIGNALWOMAN'S ROUTE
Plot the March's Act III course. A real map puzzle with real consequences for which regions
are reachable and when. **Route control as a mechanic** — this is how a player who
understands the game can partially steer their Act III.

### Q31 · COUPLING DUTY
Learn to work the couplings. It's a job. You do it for twenty minutes.
**No lore, no character revelation, no reward beyond an achievement.** It exists so the
March feels like a home, and playtest confirms it works better than any dialogue could.

## ACT III

### Q32 · VEY'S WRONG CHART
One orbit around nothing. Nine years of checking. **Delivers F12 on all routes** (the
shared vessel — Stage 2 §11.1). The catalogue name is **Corrin Vane**.

### Q33 · THE PAGE EDDA WON'T TOUCH ★⚠
**Outcomes.** Leave it *(she keeps her composure; her final scene is her best)* / read it
yourself *(you learn Merit's request; Edda is unchanged)* / **force her to read it**
*(she does; she is not the same; her final scene is two lines)*.

### Q34 · FERRIS TAKES TOO MANY ⚠
A boy picking Ash Garden flowers by the armful — the ones with his village's names, so
nobody else has to come and see them.
**Outcomes.** Stop him *(the names stay; his village comes; it is unbearable and correct)* /
help him *(the beds are stripped; the names regrow in a week; he has to do it again, forever)*
/ **take him to the Gardener** *(who has been doing exactly this for two hundred years and
can tell him what it costs)*.

### Q35 · NINE DOORS ★
Find all nine keyholeless doors. **Superboss chain step 1.** The Journal has been logging
them under *Questions* since Wyndmarrow without being asked.
